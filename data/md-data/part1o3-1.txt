**Purpose:** Complete elimination of ragging; ensure physical and psychological safety of all students, especially freshers

### What Constitutes Ragging

- Teasing, treating rudely, or handling with rudeness any fresher or student
- Any act causing annoyance, hardship, psychological harm, or fear
- Forcing students to do acts they wouldn't ordinarily do
- Creating shame, torment, or embarrassment
- Showing superiority or power over other students
