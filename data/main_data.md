**R. N. G. Patel Institute of Technology (RNGPIT)**.

### **1. Institute Overview**

- **Established:** 2010
- **Location:** Bardoli-Navsari Road, Isroli, Surat, Gujarat.
- **Affiliation & Approval:** Affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU) and approved by AICTE, New Delhi.
- **Key Distinctions:** The institute is recognized for its high academic standards, often ranking in the top 10 of state-level university rankings. It is heavily focused on industry-ready professional training.

### **2. Academic Programs & Accreditation**

The institute offers several NBA-accredited undergraduate (B.E./B.Tech) programs:

- **Chemical Engineering** (Accredited up to June 2025)
- **Civil Engineering** (Accredited up to June 2025)
- **Computer Science and Engineering** (Accredited up to June 2025)
- **Electrical Engineering** (Accredited up to June 2025)
- **Mechanical Engineering** (Accredited up to June 2025)
- **Other Programs:** Also offers B.Voc, MBA, and Integrated M.Sc (IT).

### **3. Leadership Messages**

- **From the Director (Dr. Latesh Chaudhari):** Focuses on the institute's role as a "Center for Excellence." He stresses the importance of practical training, industry-institute interaction, and maintaining high standards in engineering research and development.
